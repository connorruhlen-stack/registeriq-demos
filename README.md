# RegisterIQ landing pages

Designs for the RegisterIQ landing page, published so they can be opened and scrolled through.

- **All pages:** https://connorruhlen-stack.github.io/registeriq-demos/
- **Tied up** (`fable/`): https://connorruhlen-stack.github.io/registeriq-demos/fable/
- **The week, then the manual** (`opus/`): https://connorruhlen-stack.github.io/registeriq-demos/opus/

RegisterIQ reads a shop’s till and stock records and points out what doesn’t add up. It is in
its pilot and runs on sample data, so every figure on these pages is made up and stamped
Example.

This repository holds the finished pages only. They load nothing from any other site, and they
use no cookies and no tracking. The fonts are Fixel, Recursive and Commit Mono, under the SIL
Open Font License; the licence texts are in `fonts/`, `fable/licences/` and `opus/licences/`.
